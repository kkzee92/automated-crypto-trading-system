# Trading Strategies by Asset Class

## Majors (BTC, ETH)
- **Trend Following**: Dual moving average crossover (50/200 EMA) with volume confirmation.
- **Mean Reversion**: Bollinger Band extremes in ranging markets (ADX < 25).
- Enhancements: Incorporate funding rate and open interest changes.

## Large Cap Alts
- Momentum strategies with relative strength vs BTC.
- Breakout trading on volume spikes.

## DeFi Tokens
- Combine price action with protocol metrics (TVL changes, fee revenue).
- Volatility harvesting via options-like signals or grid trading simulations.

## Stablecoins
- Cross-exchange arbitrage opportunity detection.
- Yield comparison signals.

## High Volatility / Speculative
- Extremely tight risk controls: max 1% portfolio risk per trade.
- Time-based exits and strict stop-losses.

All strategies include backtesting hooks and paper trading mode.
